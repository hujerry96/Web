Your workspace quality directly affects your productivity. Here are five affordable desk essentials with proven track records.

## 1. Adjustable Monitor Stand

Elevate your screen to eye level. A good monitor stand keeps your neck in a natural position during long work sessions.

## 2. Entry-Level Mechanical Keyboard

If you type all day, mechanical switches provide tactile feedback that membrane keyboards can't match. Entry-level options start at reasonable prices.

## 3. Wireless Mouse

Cut the cord and instantly declutter your desk. Look for ergonomic grip and long battery life.

## 4. Under-Desk Cable Tray

Messy desks are usually caused by tangled cables, not clutter. A simple cable management tray makes a surprising difference.

## 5. Adjustable Book Stand

Whether for reading or holding a tablet, a book stand keeps your head up and reduces neck strain.
