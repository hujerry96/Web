## 繁體中文版

### 1. 我們收集什麼

**本軟體（桌面應用程式）：**

- **您的數據檔案：不收集。** 所有載入、處理與繪圖均在您的電腦本機進行，
  數據檔案與圖表永遠不會上傳或傳送至任何伺服器。
- **授權資訊**：離線金鑰在本機驗證。Polar 金鑰啟用、驗證及停用會連線至
  Polar，依操作傳送金鑰、組織識別碼、版本條件與啟用識別碼；啟用時亦傳送由
  本機識別資訊衍生的機器指紋（包含於裝置標籤與 metadata）。原始硬體識別資訊
  不會直接上傳。金鑰、啟用識別碼及上次驗證日期儲存在您的電腦。
  舊 Gumroad 金鑰啟用時會傳送金鑰及商品識別碼至 Gumroad。
- **版本檢查（自動）**：啟動時查詢網站的 version.json，不傳送實驗數據。
  授權及更新服務會收到一般 HTTP 連線資訊（如 IP 位址與 User-Agent）；
  Polar 請求的 User-Agent 包含軟體名稱及版本。
- **遙測（可選，未來版本）**：若未來加入使用統計，一律為**明示同意（opt-in）**，
  僅傳送功能使用計數（例如「分峰擬合按鈕被按下 3 次」），**絕不**傳送數據內容、
  檔案名稱或任何可識別個人之資訊。

**網站（hulab.pages.dev；GitHub Pages 為備援）：**

- 正式站託管於 Cloudflare Pages；備援及下載由 GitHub 提供。服務供應商可能
  保留伺服器紀錄（含 IP 位址），請參閱 Cloudflare 與 GitHub 之隱私政策。
- 我們不使用第三方廣告追蹤器。

**購買與付款：**

- 購買流程由 Polar 或舊版 Gumroad 平台處理。您的姓名、電子郵件與付款資訊

## English Version

### 1. What We Collect

**The desktop application:**

- **Your data files: NOT collected.** All loading, processing and plotting happen
  locally on your computer. Your data files and figures are never uploaded or sent
  to any server.
- **License information**: Offline keys are verified locally. Polar activation,
  validation and deactivation send the key, organization ID, version conditions
  and activation ID as applicable. Activation also sends a derived machine
  fingerprint in the device label and metadata, not raw hardware identifiers.
  The key, activation ID and last validation date are stored on your computer.
  Legacy Gumroad activation sends the license key and product ID to Gumroad.
- **Version check (automatic)**: The app requests the website's version.json on
  startup without uploading experimental data. Licensing and update providers
  receive ordinary HTTP connection information such as IP address and User-Agent;
  the Polar User-Agent includes the application name and version.
- **Telemetry (optional, future versions)**: If usage statistics are added in the
  future, they will be strictly **opt-in** and limited to feature-usage counters
  (e.g., "the peak-fitting button was pressed 3 times"). We will **never** transmit
  data content, file names, or any personally identifiable information.

**The website (hulab.pages.dev; GitHub Pages backup):**

- Cloudflare Pages hosts the production site; GitHub provides backup hosting and
  downloads. Providers may retain server logs including IP addresses; refer to
  the Cloudflare and GitHub privacy policies.
- We do not use third-party advertising trackers.

**Purchases and payments:**

- Purchases are processed by Polar or the legacy Gumroad platform. Your
  name, email and payment details are handled by that platform under its own
  privacy policy. We only receive order information (e.g., product and quantity)
  to issue licenses.

### 2. How We Use Information

We use the information above solely to: deliver update notifications, issue and
manage licenses, and respond to your support requests.

### 3. Information Sharing

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal data. We do not share your information
with third parties except as described in this policy (payment, licensing and hosting services) or as
required by law.

### 4. Data Retention

We do not retain your data files. Order records related to licensing are kept per
the platform's policy; you may contact us at any time to request deletion.

### 5. Children's Privacy

The Software is intended for professional and academic use. It is not designed for
children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

### 6. Changes

Material changes to this policy will be announced on our website or in software
release notes.
