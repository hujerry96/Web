# Materials Science Study Guide: The Notebook That Actually Helps You Pass

Most materials science textbooks drown you in derivations. This study guide does the opposite — it gives you the **core concepts, the formulas you actually need, and space to practice** until they stick.

## Who It's For

- Undergraduate engineering students taking their first materials science course
- Self-learners preparing for exams or brushing up before grad school
- Anyone who wants a portable, no-fluff companion to their textbook

## What's Inside

1. **Crystal structures** — BCC, FCC, HCP, atomic packing factor, Miller indices with worked examples
2. **Defects and diffusion** — vacancies, dislocations, Fick's laws made intuitive
3. **Phase diagrams** — reading binary diagrams, lever rule, eutectics step by step
4. **Mechanical properties** — stress–strain, hardness, fatigue, and fracture basics
5. **Practice pages** — fill-in prompts and quick quizzes after every section

## Why a Printable Notebook?

Active recall beats passive reading. Each chapter ends with a **fill-in page** so you write the concept in your own words — the single most effective study habit for technical subjects.
